# Logo concepts

Exploration only: pick a direction, then a single refined mark lands in
the app (nothing here is wired into any build). Preview by opening the
SVGs in a browser; each is a standalone 256x256 app-icon frame on the
app's dark surface (`#181818`) with the brand red (`#FF0033`).

- `01-bold-m.svg`: heavy rounded M, red on dark. Loudest, most legible
  at small sizes.
- `02-eq-bars-m.svg`: seven EQ bars in an M silhouette (white center
  bar). Most "music app" read.
- `03-note.svg`: classic eighth note in brand red.
- `04-play-m.svg`: white M with a red play triangle nested in the
  middle valley.
- `05-waveform-m.svg`: symmetric waveform trace that dips like an M.
- `06-monoline-m.svg`: thin white M. Quietest, most premium feel.
- `07-hex-m.svg`: red hexagon badge with the dark surface knocked out
  as an M.
- `08-vinyl-m.svg`: vinyl disc with grooves, red label, white mini M.
  Nod to the record-collection side of the app.
- `09-gradient-m.svg`: bold M fading red to pink on near-black.
- `10-pixel-m.svg`: 8-bit block M with one white pixel. Playful.
- `11-wordmark.svg`: `yutu` in white plus `mux` in red, bundled-Inter
  fallback stack (rendering varies by system fonts).
- `12-orbit-m.svg`: red M with a white orbit ring, planet vibe.
- `13-rings-m.svg`: white M broadcasting red signal rings, all centered
  on the canvas.
- `33-rings-bold.svg`: heavier rings and a bolder M.
- `34-rings-dashed.svg`: dotted signal rings, dash periods tiled to
  divide each circumference exactly (12/16/20 dashes) with a slimmer M.
- `35-rings-inverse.svg`: red M inside white rings.
- `36-ring-solo.svg`: single thick ring, minimal.
- `37-rings-play.svg`: play triangle at the broadcast center.
- `38-rings-note.svg`: eighth note at the broadcast center.
- `39-rings-gradient.svg`: rings fading red to dark on near-black.
- `40-rings-square.svg`: concentric rounded-square waves.
- `41-rings-dot.svg`: red broadcast dot nested in the M valley.
- `42-rings-mono.svg`: all-white single-color version.
- `14-headphones.svg`: red headphone silhouette.
- `15-cassette.svg`: retro tape with dark reels on a red shell.
- `16-piano.svg`: white keys with the accidentals in brand red.
- `17-speaker.svg`: red speaker cone with radiating sound arcs.
- `18-treble-swirl.svg`: single-line treble-clef abstraction.
- `19-bolt-m.svg`: sharp mitered zigzag M, high energy.
- `20-chevrons.svg`: triple fast-forward chevrons.
- `21-ring-m.svg`: white M inside a broken red ring.
- `22-peaks-m.svg`: twin mountain peaks with snow caps on a red range.
- `23-heartbeat.svg`: red heart outline with a white pulse trace.
- `24-gem.svg`: faceted red gem, pure geometric mark with no letter.
- `25-links.svg`: interlocking red and white rings.
- `26-y.svg`: bold red Y for yutu.
- `27-moon-note.svg`: white crescent with a small red note, late-night
  listening.
- `28-flame.svg`: red flame with a white core.
- `29-origami-play.svg`: red play triangle with a folded paper corner.
- `30-dots-m.svg`: dot-matrix M with one white dot.
- `31-infinity.svg`: endless red loop, queue-that-never-ends nod.
- `32-crown-m.svg`: red crown with a white base bar.
